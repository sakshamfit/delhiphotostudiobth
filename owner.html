About the Owner

The man behind the lens

Shahnawaz Ali

Founder & Chief Photographer · Delhi Photo Studio BTH

His vision

Shahnawaz founded Delhi Photo Studio BTH in Bettiah, Bihar, and has spent more than a decade photographing weddings across the state and beyond — from quiet registry marriages to week-long celebrations. He still shoots every wedding himself, from the first phone call to the final album, and takes the same care with a two-hour portrait sitting as with a three-day baraat. To talk about your day with him directly, say hello on WhatsApp.
