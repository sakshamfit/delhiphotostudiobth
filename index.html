About

Delhi Photo Studio is a wedding photography and cinematography studio based in Bettiah, Bihar — rated 4.5 stars across 173 reviews. We document the whole arc of a celebration, from bridal mornings and baraats to the pheras and the dance floor after, in candid frames, traditional portraits, and cinematic films. This wedding album holds that work the way we make it — page after page of your favourite celebrations — and to start your own chapter, say hello on WhatsApp.
